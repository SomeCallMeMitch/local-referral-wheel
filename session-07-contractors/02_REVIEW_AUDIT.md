## Codes

Positive: R responsiveness; L listening; A useful advice; B budget respect; P punctuality; C communication; H respect for home; K cleanliness; X accuracy/detail; I issue resolution; W warranty/post-sale; U repeat use; F explicit referral; O owner involvement.

Negative: MA missed appointment; PR pressure; SC surprise charge/scope; PD property damage; UW unfinished work; DC delay without communication; WR warranty resistance; NR failure to respond. A blank is unknown, not a clean finding. A service delay alone is not automatically DC. Reviews about owners are not independent proof of legal ownership.
