## Neutral questions for a possible later owner conversation

These are due-diligence questions, not outreach copy or a recruitment plan. No conversation is authorized by this deliverable.

1. Who currently owns the business, makes relationship decisions and handles customer concerns? Which legal entity contracts with the homeowner?
2. Which actual job types and project sizes are ideal? Which should never be referred? Are minimum charges and travel terms clear?
3. How many Benicia households did you serve over the last twelve months, and what kinds of work did they buy? How seasonal was that?
4. What is the present scheduling range by job type? Is additional qualified work useful, or would it displace existing clients?
5. How do you explain options when a client has a limited budget or should postpone work?
6. Describe a recent mistake, delay or unexpected condition: who told the client, who paid for the correction, and what changed afterward?
7. Who owns callbacks and warranty issues? What happens if the original employee or installer leaves?
8. What is included in preparation, furniture moving, installation, cleanup and final walkthrough? What typically creates scope confusion?
9. Which adjacent categories do you already perform, sell, subcontract or refer? Where is there an actual gap?
10. What existing referral relationships work well? Would an additional introduction source add anything useful?
11. How do you obtain permission before sharing customer details? What minimum information is appropriate to share?
12. Would a brief weekly status discussion be useful? Could basic pending/closed/collected outcomes be confirmed without adopting a new CRM?
13. Which parts depend personally on the owner, and which can another designated person handle consistently?
14. What would make an introduced customer a poor fit? How should that be communicated without pressure?
15. After separate legal advice, what arrangement—if any—would be acceptable and economically incremental? No specific payment is presumed.
