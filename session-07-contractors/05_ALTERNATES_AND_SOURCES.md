## Specific exclusions and cautions

- **Handymen LLC:** dated official suspension beats its otherwise active service pages. Its own explanation cites reduced housing mobility and rising operating costs; this is one operator's account, not proof of the whole Benicia market's condition.
- **Discovery Shutters & Shadings:** [official site](https://www.discoveryshutter.com/) describes Stockton/valley service. No convincing Benicia travel/service evidence found; excluded geographically.
- **National Footprints Floors ratings:** [Benicia service page](https://footprintsfloors.com/fairfield-napa/cities-we-serve/benicia-ca) is geographic evidence, but national review counts cannot establish the local operator's quality. Not ranked without local owner/license/review separation.
- **Gil's review widget:** [company review page](https://www.gilscarpets.com/about/reviews/) linked a widget with a rating lower-bound filter of four. Selected visibility cannot demonstrate absence of poor reviews. [Chamber profile](https://cca.martinezchamber.com/gils-carpets-inc-_5005622_3_1_20019200_memberprofile2.aspx) and [BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/martinez/profile/flooring-contractors/gils-carpets-1116-46278) also differ in entity presentation; verify before relying on legal identity.
- **BHIC in the wheel:** broad cabinets/flooring/paint/design scope can remove the reason for separate introductions. Possible high client value does not equal clean partner boundaries.
- **B'Santi lodging/STR:** exclude guest-experience reviews from a residential cleaning quality score and exclude turnover/guest emergency support from the founder's role.
- **Award/SEO directories:** “best” badges, AI summaries and city landing pages alone are not reviewed customer-care evidence.
- **Emergency trades:** no emergency plumbing, restoration, lockout or urgent HVAC in the recommended configurations. This is a fit exclusion, not a judgment of those businesses.
- **Solano namesakes:** do not mix Fairfield Solano Painting Bros with similarly named out-of-area Solano painting companies or their license numbers.

## Source-only hierarchy — no assumed paying recipients

1. **Living Lite Organized:** best documented early-needs discovery; conditional Benicia eligibility and existing-vendor overlap.
2. **Simplified Spaces:** best directly local organizing fallback; independent-quality uncertainty substantial.
3. **Shandrika Powell:** strongest broad hub evidence, but already coordinates many trades and is separately regulated; weak incremental founder rationale.
4. **Michael Trahan:** credible local professional/network identity; insufficient original client audit to rank alongside Shandrika.
5. **Residential cleaner such as B'Santi:** potentially repeated household visibility; cleaning-only evidence and consent practices must be separated from other operations.
6. **Caring Transitions local franchise:** a geographically relevant downsizing lead, not yet an evidence-backed finalist.

Source partners can create most of the value. Nothing here assumes they owe introductions, will accept reciprocal business, or should pay the founder. The same household's income/home value does not demonstrate willingness to purchase adjacent services.
