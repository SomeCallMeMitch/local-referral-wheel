# Benicia contractor and adjacent-partner evaluation
Public-source research checked 2026-09-14. No business was contacted. Recommendations concern research priority and customer fit, not partner selection or permission to pay compensation.

## Recommendation

**The selected pair is stronger than the wheel as a business hypothesis.** Home Living Window Fashions (HLWF) plus Hire My Husband (HMH) combines a specialized purchase with planned home preparation. The best provisional three-business configuration adds Living Lite Organized (LLO) as an early source. The best four-business variation adds Benicia Floor Company (BFC), but the fourth position is substantially less verified.

**No three- or four-business configuration clears the entire requested verification standard.** This is a completed public-source evaluation with explicit access limits, not a completed due-diligence clearance. The reviews establish credible candidates; they do not establish willingness, spare capacity, Benicia lead volume, margins, or sustainable founder income.

## What changed from Session 6

- Retain HLWF as the most distinctive contractor anchor. The correct company Houzz profile has seven reviews; the separate Shem Isaac designer profile has none. The latter must not be used to dismiss the former.
- Promote HMH from satellite to selected-pair contender, but only for scheduled work with defined limits. Do not put a second full-service painter into the same core by default.
- LLO challenges Simplified Spaces on evidence depth. Its county-wide service claim is meaningful, but cannot substitute for proof of actual Benicia work.
- BFC remains the first narrow flooring hypothesis, not a verified finalist. An undated Instagram closure notice includes pandemic wording; current closure is **not established**.
- Solano Painting Bros becomes an alternate: an identifiable historical correction episode is valuable, but most accessible reviews are old and entity continuity needs reconciliation.
- Floors on First is not a clear upgrade: its accessible original review page includes serious historical service complaints and window-treatment overlap.
- Shandrika Powell is a source/hub hypothesis rather than an assumed paying recipient. Her broad existing coordination role creates a strong substitution risk.
- B'Santi's residential cleaning evidence must be isolated from commercial, short-term-rental and lodging evidence.

## Why not the obvious painting–flooring–blinds wheel?

The services fit a room refresh, but homeowners do not automatically buy all three. Flooring and paint can exhaust the budget before custom shades are considered. Existing provider networks already do some of the proposed work. Accessible evidence is also uneven: attractive headline ratings conceal old reviews, duplicate syndication and inaccessible low scores.

A better customer outcome may be an introduction that prevents an unnecessary purchase. That is consistent with the founder's care preference but conflicts with treating every introduction as monetizable.

## Founder fit and economic conclusion

A weekly administrative cadence is plausible only if partners handle scoping, estimates, scheduling, technical advice, payment, warranties and complaints directly. Founder work could remain permission and status tracking, remembering deferred needs, checking whether an introduction was acknowledged, and recording the outcome. It cannot promise construction coordination or urgent recovery.

Relationships may remain useful after the businesses know each other; the founder's incremental value is less certain. Repeatedly collecting payment for relationships that now run themselves is the central commercial weakness, independent of legality.

At hypothetical collected compensation of $750 per win, $4,000/$7,500/$10,000 monthly gross receipts require **6/10/14 collected wins**. Public evidence does not establish that this small configuration can supply even the first level consistently. These are thresholds, not proposed, lawful or accepted fees.
