# Pairwise referral map
All edges below are **hypothesized customer needs**, not observed referrals between these named businesses. No relationship, consent or willingness is assumed. Timing is a planning estimate, not a quoted lead time.

H = Home Living Window Fashions; M = Hire My Husband; O = Living Lite Organized (Benicia service conditional); F = Benicia Floor Company (current qualification conditional).

## What remains for the founder — and what disappears

| Pair | Useful weekly work, if customers and partners want it | Likely value concentration | Can relationship stay useful after introductions? | Is ongoing founder compensation supported by evidence? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H–M | Record consent, readiness and acknowledgment; remember deferred window work; update pending/closed/paid status under a later valid arrangement | M may supply more households; H may receive larger specialty orders | Yes, direct bilateral coordination is straightforward | No. The pair may become self-sufficient quickly |
| H–O | Preserve customer-approved room goal and deferred timing; ensure no unwanted handoff | O likely provides early detection and most source value | Yes, if geography and existing-vendor gaps fit | No. Tracking alone may be too little incremental value |
| M–O | Record only whether access is ready and recipient acknowledged; avoid becoming move scheduler | Balanced needs but O could already own the entire referral function | Yes, strongly; operational details belong directly to providers | No. Redundancy is especially likely |
| H–F | Remember a later sun/privacy decision; record a customer-authorized outcome | F→H likely stronger than reverse | Yes; repeated flooring clients can learn the specialist directly | No. Need may be too infrequent for a weekly paid layer |
| M–F | Keep the correct business attached to the agreed introduction; note exceptions for provider follow-up | Larger F revenue may arise from smaller M source work | Yes, with clear scope boundaries | No. Administrative involvement must not become regulated coordination |
| O–F | Record the customer's chosen phase; avoid contacting them repeatedly during a sensitive transition | O likely does more early decision work | Yes; organizer can refer directly | No. Relocation sensitivity also raises support cost |

Do not impose equal referral quotas. A source-only O can be valuable without paying or receiving equal business. A direct H–M pair may offer better customer care and less founder work than the four-business variation. None of these advantages establishes a durable revenue right.

The customer—not the referral configuration—determines whether, when and how much to buy. No pair should be justified by a presumed commission, a shared wealthy ZIP code, or a provider's size.
