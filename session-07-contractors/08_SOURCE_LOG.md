# Source log and research method

All access/check dates are **2026-09-14**. “Indexed” means a search engine exposed text from the named page; it is weaker than a full original page inspection. A failed access is logged as a failure, not as verification. The review audit records actual readable populations. Raw review texts and personal addresses are not copied.

Types: R = review evidence on an independent platform; R derivative = syndicated/aggregated review evidence, not another independent household; C = company/owner claim or provider-selected content; D = third-party business/member directory, not a state record; P = public-record resource; E = editorial cost research or reporting; I = analyst inference. One source can contain multiple types.

## Access limits and deduplication

Direct Yelp review pages linked from company/aggregator pages failed to render usable review populations. Google original review populations were not fully accessible; derivatives are clearly labeled. HomeAdvisor pagination exposed first-page text but additional pages were not reliably retrievable. The HMH page-two attempt and targeted low-score searches did not expose the hidden adverse records. No login, paywall bypass, customer impersonation or form submission was used.

Houzz was retried after a transient timeout and provided much better original text. Dates often disappear in its rendering; that does not make those reviews recent. Shandrika's full rendered set was read, while Zillow provided truncated excerpts only.
