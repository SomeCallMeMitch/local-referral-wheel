# Referral Growth Network --- Research & Astra Handoff

## Purpose

This repository-ready research pack is designed to be handed to an
Astra/strategy chat and turned into a business plan, operating system,
curriculum, SaaS specification, and launch plan.

The core concept combines three proven ideas:

1.  **Referral Wheel** --- a deliberately constructed ecosystem of
    complementary, noncompeting businesses that can recognize trigger
    events and refer business to one another.
2.  **Peer Advisory / Growth Council** --- a paid, facilitated group
    where business owners work on growth, profitability, decisions,
    accountability, and implementation.
3.  **SaaS Growth Dashboard** --- software that tracks goals, referrals,
    actions, growth levers, experiments, outcomes, member value, and
    potentially AI-generated recommendations/assets.

The opportunity is not to clone FRAP, BNI, Vistage, TAB, EO, or anyone
else's proprietary system. The opportunity is to combine
well-established business concepts into an original product with a
stronger implementation and measurement layer.

## Central Hypothesis

A local business owner may be reluctant to pay monthly merely for
"networking" or "coaching." A combined system can provide multiple
independent reasons to remain a member:

-   qualified referrals;
-   strategic relationships;
-   facilitated peer advice;
-   structured growth work;
-   actual implementation assets;
-   accountability;
-   measurable ROI;
-   software that records and compounds the value;
-   access to vetted specialists and opportunities.

This creates a potential flywheel:

**Members → relationships → referrals → measurable revenue → stronger
retention → better case studies → easier recruiting → more members →
more network value.**

## Recommended strategic posture

Do **not** begin by building a large SaaS product or a 12-month course.
Begin by proving that a small number of qualified owners will join and
participate. Operate the first cohort manually enough to learn what
members actually use. Productize repeated behavior after evidence
appears.

## Important IP boundary

Use public business principles and independently designed frameworks. Do
not copy proprietary FRAP curriculum, Vistage issue-processing
methodology, BNI materials, TAB materials, trademarks, scripts,
templates, or protected content.
