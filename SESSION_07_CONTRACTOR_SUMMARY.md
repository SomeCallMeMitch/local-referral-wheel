## Economics and legal separation

At invented collected compensation points of $250/$750/$1,250, monthly gross-receipts targets require:

| Target | $250 | $750 | $1,250 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $4,000 | 16 | 6 | 4 |
| $7,500 | 30 | 10 | 6 |
| $10,000 | 40 | 14 | 8 |

These are thresholds, not lawful or available fees. The public evidence does not substantiate a consistent $4,000, $7,500 or $10,000 monthly outcome. Small cleaning/handyman tickets are especially weak as assumed paying recipients. Source partners need not pay or receive equal referrals.

Contractors remain **awaiting legal analysis**. Expressly non-construction organizing/cleaning are **likely non-contractor category**, not a blanket exemption for add-ons. Real-estate activity is **separately regulated**. No commission workaround, dues model, contract or outreach plan was created.
