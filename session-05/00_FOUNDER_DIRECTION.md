## Correction to Session 4 emphasis

Session 4 fairly explored independent SaaS but overemphasized customer software adoption in relation to this founder-operated concept. Most participants need only contribute suitable introductions and give brief accurate status updates; the founder maintains the system. A partner not logging into a portal is not product failure here. Refusing to disclose a fee-eligible outcome or payment is a different problem, still relevant to commissions.

The founder's new preferences change which options deserve immediate conceptual depth. They do not erase the standalone software branch or establish that a commission-funded network has been selected as a final business. Retainers and monthly dues remain historical comparators, not the proposed income engine.
