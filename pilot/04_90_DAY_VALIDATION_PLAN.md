# Validation plan: exploration followed by one selected pilot
These are proposed decision rules, not evidence or industry norms. Timing: prelaunch discovery/recruitment occurs BEFORE 90 delivery days. Do not call week-12 renewal a 90-day membership if recruitment consumed the first month.

## Stage 0: explore before selecting
Reconstruct original relationship-fee history, interview 12 owners, compare A/E/C with actual cost examples, confirm founder role/geography and fee constraints. Choose one coherent offer. Defaults below refer to A with eight members; E overrides appear afterward. No outreach or pilot launch was performed in Session 02.

## A stage gates
Rates use the actual enrolled cohort denominator; target-8 counts are illustrative. Missing records are missing, not successes. Pause for severe trust/safety/privacy failures regardless of commercial performance.

| Stage | Metric / observation window | Success | Warning | Kill/pivot current offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Prospect list | 40 reachable candidates in one service area before interviews | >=3 viable candidates for each of 8 scopes | Thin coverage in 1–2 scopes | Fewer than 6 adjacent viable scopes; change architecture |
| 2 Discovery | 12 qualified interviews, recent real events and current pain | >=6 concrete events; >=4 owners with coordination pain | Events plausible but no current pain/spend | Fewer than 3 actual events; stop assuming wheel density |
| 3 Founding pitch | 10 qualified written offers, same terms, 21 days | >=6 request a concrete paid start | 3–5 serious prospects | <=2; revise job/price/segment before recruiting harder |
| 4 Payment | Paid first month, not verbal interest, within 21 days of offers | >=6 pay; target 8 | 3–5 pay: extend at most 14 days after diagnosing | <=2 or still <6 after extension: don't launch A |
| 5 Onboarding | Profiles, checks, response owner before day 1 | 100% complete before routing | Up to 2 incomplete; delay their participation | Fewer than 6 ready; delay/refund rather than fake launch |
| 6 First meeting | Attendance by day 7 | >=75% present | 50–74% | <50%: pause cadence/offer and investigate |
| 7 Referral behavior | By day 30, accepted qualified intros and breadth | >=1 accepted intro/member on group average; >=50% members touched | Volume exists but concentrated | Zero accepted intros: pause network premise |
| 8 Implementation | One bounded core exercise by day 30 | >=60% complete one eligible follow-up action | 30–59% | <30%: remove/redesign exercise; don't kill useful referral model solely for this |
| 9 Day-30 review | Records + service adherence | >=90% active statuses current; >=90% timely acknowledgments | Either 70–89% | Either <70% after reminder cycle: pause routing to failing providers |
| 10 Day-60 review | Participation and useful outcomes across cohort | >=75% have sent/received accepted intro; >=50% verify useful outcome | 50–74% touched or <50% useful outcomes | <50% touched and no credible near-term pipeline: redesign/stop |
| 11 Day-90 renewal | Actual payment at disclosed continuation terms | >=75% renew (6/8), plus economic/trust gates | 50–74% renew | <50% renew: stop current configuration |

An accepted intro is consented, fits recipient scope, passes basic quality gate and is acknowledged as a real opportunity; it is not a sale. Useful outcome = accepted fit appointment, completed relevant job, documented time saved or implemented improvement, reported separately by type; no blending into financial ROI. At day 60 explicitly count wins, not only activity.

## Economic and concentration overrides
Steady-state after-cost contribution/hour target >=$50 by month 3; $35–49 warning; <$35 for two successive months redesign/stop unless founder explicitly chooses a bounded learning subsidy. Evaluate first-cohort startup costs separately as in pilot/03.
If one source generates >50% of accepted introductions, label concentration and recruit/test alternatives before expansion. Total revenue does not override a majority of members reporting no value.

Day 75: offer renewal with clear price; day 90: collect or record cancellation. If longer sales cycles prevent outcome measurement, extend observation without calling pipeline realized value. A full-price renewed cohort and healthy service behavior are necessary before another chapter.

## E overrides: relationship stewardship / success fees
No requirement for mandatory group attendance; measure source/recipient check-ins and completed handoffs instead. Before live fees: specify jurisdiction, payer, fee base, covered relationships, repeated-deal treatment, attribution window and termination, and obtain arrangement-specific review.

Start 3 managed relationships with at least 2 independent sources and 3 recipients; no claim of network economics from one favored pair. Obtain private agreement on deal reporting and fee reconciliation. Follow at least 3 eligible deals through actual fee collection; target >=90% due-fee dollars collected within 30 days of due date. Warning: discrepancies above 10% or reconciliation >30 minutes/deal; pause invoicing disputed amounts and redesign. Kill fee version if arrangement not permissible, recipients cannot verify basis, or fee erases economic value.

Measure ongoing coordination performed and whether both parties still want it after they know each other. Apply actual funnel and payment lag to break-even formula in pilot/03. A signed relationship agreement alone is not revenue proof.

## C micro-test before a 90-day club
Four paying firms, one specific implementation sprint, no broad curriculum. Success: >=3 implement and request paid continuation; support cap from C11. Failure may indicate onboarding/data problems rather than no demand, so distinguish inability from unwillingness.

## Record every decision
At each gate: numerator, denominator, time window, evidence link, decision, responsible owner, next date, deviation reason. A changed threshold must be dated before evaluating the next period, not silently changed to make results look successful.

For E2, use the ongoing-relationship scope and duration alternatives in strategy/08 rather than silently adopting E1 first-job-only terms. Measure completed partner reviews, resolved handoff failures and persistence of bidirectional opportunities alongside collected fees. Continued activity under a relationship agreement is not by itself proof the steward caused every deal.
