# Founding offer: worked example, not a launch decision

> Latest direction (2026-09-13): Vallejo, California and surrounding cities; founder role is connector; every fee/amount remains illustrative, with participating-business profitability first. See [current connector and fee-design update](../strategy/09_VALLEJO_CONNECTOR_AND_FEE_DESIGN.md). This update overrides conflicting earlier defaults.
This document makes baseline A concrete enough to compare with E/C. Prices and terms are hypotheses. No prospects have been contacted and no offer has been accepted.

## Positioning
A small, managed circle of complementary local service businesses that helps trusted introductions turn into suitable work.

Headline: **Build reliable referral partnerships without adding another weekly meeting.**

Three benefits: clearer partner fit; prompt warm handoffs with less chasing; a private record of what happened so membership can be evaluated honestly.

For established residential service businesses with good service, capacity, real household customer contact and a reliable response owner. Not for businesses seeking guaranteed leads, aggressive selling to members, an outsourced marketing department or broad executive coaching.

## Exactly what a founding member receives
| Item | Proposed specification |
|---|---|
| Size | Target 8, minimum 6, maximum 10 for 90-day pilot |
| Geography | One overlapping service area, practical roughly 30-minute meeting access; actual city unselected |
| Category | One defined service scope per cohort; written exceptions for capacity/customer choice; no outside-network restriction |
| Onboarding | 45-minute profile conversation, verification and introductions to two adjacent partners |
| Meetings | One 75-minute session monthly; 3 sessions in delivery period |
| Format | 10 minutes verified outcomes; 15 minutes trigger examples; 20 minutes handoff problems; 20 minutes pair/next-action work; 10 minutes commitments |
| Narrow implementation | In one session, use the 20-minute work block to implement referral/estimate follow-up; no additional workshop promise |
| Directory | Shared member-only profiles, scope, area, contact owner, capacity and trigger examples |
| Referrals | Permissioned named introductions; basic fit check; recipient can decline |
| Tracking | Minimal private status log; optional private dollar values; monthly individual summary |
| Accountability | One useful action/person/month, owner/date; short weekly coordinator reminder where needed |
| Between meetings | Shared member email channel for partner questions; coordinator replies within 2 business days; no emergency/on-call service |
| Facilitator responsibilities | Profiles, matching, meeting prep, status follow-up, private summaries and bounded conflict triage |
| Member responsibilities | Attend at least 2 of 3 meetings; acknowledge referrals in 1 business day; update active status weekly; respect consent; give useful help when possible |
| Limits | No guaranteed number/value of referrals, no requirement to buy from members, no referral quota |
| Price | $199/month, charged monthly for 90 days; no deal fee |
| Exit | Cancel before next monthly charge; no annual lock-in; refund if group fails to launch; clear conduct/termination terms required |
| Founding difference | Shape the process, limited cohort, feedback expected; price fixed through day 90 only |
| After pilot | Explicit renewal consent at disclosed price; no automatic unannounced increase |

## Included in pilot
Member qualification, narrow profiles, consented handoffs, three meetings, status follow-up, one core-job implementation exercise, private value reviews and a renewal decision.

## Explicitly deferred
Custom SaaS, AI advice, broad financial scorecards, experts, coaching packages, large workshops, marketplace, agency services, paid-referral arrangements in A, licensing and expansion. E would require fee reconciliation from day one; that cannot be deferred if testing E.

## First-call pitch structure
First understand a recent referral experience, then confirm the specific problem. Explain the narrow service and time commitment, show a sample handoff and monthly report, state price and limits plainly, ask whether to receive a written invitation. Do not claim savings or earnings without evidence.

## Objections
| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| I already get referrals | Then the question is whether partner gaps or dropped handoffs cost you useful work. If not, this may add little. |
| I tried BNI | What specifically failed? This tests smaller event-adjacent matching and managed handoffs, but referral quality still depends on people. |
| No time for another networking group | The proposal is 75 minutes monthly plus actual referral response. We will measure the extra time. |
| I have a coach | This offers partner flow and coordination. It may complement coaching; it does not replace it. |
| I won't disclose my numbers | Full business financials are not required. Referral status is required; job dollar values are optional in this dues-only offer. |
| Why $300–$500? | This baseline is $199. Higher prices would require a demonstrably different service and proof; they are not justified by ambition. |
| Can you guarantee referrals? | No. We can define service standards and let you decide whether continued membership is worth it. |
| What if service is poor? | We check fit/references, investigate reports and pause/remove providers under written standards; we cannot guarantee their work. |
| Why pay dues and a success fee? | A has no success fee. E would need separate clear terms and a total-cost comparison; it is not an undisclosed upsell. |

## Alternate E1 concept card
A managed referral desk with free introducers and 4–8 paying recipients. Test $99/month plus 5% of eligible first-job collected revenue, capped at $500/job; no recurring/lifetime fee, no fee on excluded existing opportunities. Compare with $0 plus 10%, a fixed collected-win fee, and A's dues-only option. These are economic hypotheses; geography, sector and exact activities must be reviewed before quoting a live fee offer.

Risk reversal should protect against non-delivery of our service or failure to launch, not promise customer sales. A deposit that is refundable remains a liability until earned and is weaker evidence than a completed paid month.
