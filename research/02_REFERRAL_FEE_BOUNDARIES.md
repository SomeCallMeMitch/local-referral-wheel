# Referral-fee boundaries that change concept selection

> Latest direction (2026-09-13): Vallejo, California and surrounding cities; founder role is connector; every fee/amount remains illustrative, with participating-business profitability first. See [current connector and fee-design update](../strategy/09_VALLEJO_CONNECTOR_AND_FEE_DESIGN.md). This update overrides conflicting earlier defaults.
Reviewed 2026-09-13. California is now the relevant starting jurisdiction, following the founder's Vallejo clarification. This is a decision dependency, not an arrangement-specific legal opinion.

## What the primary sources establish
- **Mortgage settlement:** CFPB explains that RESPA Section 8 restricts giving or accepting things of value for settlement-service referrals involving federally related mortgage loans. Disclosure alone does not make an impermissible arrangement permissible. [CFPB FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act-faqs/).
- **Legal services:** ABA Model Rule 7.2 limits compensation for recommendations and specifies conditions for certain reciprocal arrangements, including non-exclusivity and client awareness. It is a model rule; applicable state rules must be checked. [ABA Model Rule 7.2](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_2_advertising.ssologout/).
- **California contracting:** BPC 7157 contains restrictions on inducements and compensation in covered contracting situations, with specific exceptions. Its text is not a blanket conclusion that every marketing/referral service is either permitted or prohibited. Exact activities, licensing status and payment parties matter. California is the intended starting jurisdiction; exact activities and payment arrangements remain unreviewed. [California BPC 7157](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7157.).
- **Pricing discussions:** FTC states businesses must set prices independently rather than agree with competitors. A peer group should not become a channel for coordinated prices or other competitive terms. [FTC price-fixing guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/advice-guidance/competition-guidance/guide-antitrust-laws/dealings-competitors/price-fixing).

Insurance, healthcare, investment advice, securities/business brokerage and licensed professional services need their own applicable rules checked before fee testing. They were not comprehensively researched here. No claim of legal clearance is made for any proposed seat.

## Implications for exploration
1. A pure referral-fee wheel and a mixed professional-services council are not economically interchangeable.
2. Fixed fees, "marketing fees," membership credits, free seats and gifts can still raise concerns depending on the arrangement. Renaming compensation is not a solution.
3. A local home-service network may still need contractor-specific review. Avoid concluding that removing lenders makes all fees safe.
4. Nonexclusive customer choice and narrow in-group scope help the product design but are not legal safe harbors.
5. A relationship-stewardship fee must correspond to an actually permissible arrangement; continued coordination does not automatically authorize a percentage of every deal.
6. Keep price/cost work private and independent. No group agreement on rates, margins, territories or customer allocation.

## Concrete review brief before any fee offer
Provide counsel: jurisdiction; each party's industry/license; who pays whom; whether operator solicits, recommends, negotiates or merely introduces; whether activity touches regulated transactions; exact fee base/rate/cap; first vs repeat deals; ongoing coordination work; consent/disclosure wording; data flows; treatment after exit; and contract/payment flow. Ask which structures are permissible and what changes are necessary.

This review is not needed to brainstorm. It is needed before claiming the fee arrangement can be sold or operated. Continue non-fee concept analysis while the question is open.
