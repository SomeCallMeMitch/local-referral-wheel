## Table stakes and credible differences
Trust, noncompetition where appropriate, accountability, member profiles and referral reporting already exist in the market. BNI also advertises member technology, so "we have an app" is not a credible distinction. [BNI experience](https://www.bni.com/the-bni-experience/) and [specialty policy](https://www.bni.com/the-latest/blog-news/why-bni-allows-only-one-profession-per-chapter/).

Vistage describes 12–16 noncompeting leaders, confidentiality, facilitated peer discussion and one-to-one coaching. This supports advisory as an established category; it does not prove demand for our local hybrid. [Vistage peer groups](https://vistage.com/membership/peer-advisory-groups/).

TAB's current description includes local boards and individual coaching, with locally varying monthly fees. Its older $600–900 range is contextual, not a verified quote for the intended pilot's market. [Current TAB description](https://www.thealternativeboard.com/blog/how-the-alternative-board-works-peer-boards-coaching-and-the-franchise-model), [older guide](https://www.thealternativeboard.com/blog/guide-to-finding-right-peer-advisory-group).

Chambers can provide networking, visibility and local resources. An operator must explain why managed introductions add value beyond these benefits. [U.S. Chamber overview of local membership benefits](https://www.uschamber.com/co/run/finance/benefits-of-joining-local-chamber-of-commerce).

EOS is not interchangeable with a peer group; its own practitioner explanation distinguishes organization-wide operating work from owner peer discussions. Do not reproduce its methods. [EOS practitioner comparison](https://implementer.eosworldwide.com/implementer-post/ceo-peer-groups-and-eos-the-choice-is-yours/).

Our **hypothesized** distinction is a small set of event-adjacent partners with active handoff/relationship coordination, or a narrow completed-work service. Neither is inherently unique or defensible. Demonstrated response quality, incremental opportunities and operator reliability could make them useful.

## Claims not to make
"First referral intelligence network"; guaranteed leads/profit; better ROI than BNI/TAB without comparable evidence; competitors do not implement/measure; dues automatically pay for themselves; no legal issues because fees are small; exclusive access to a whole local market.

Potential underserved position: established service owners who want useful partner flow but find recurring networking time costly. This is an inference to test, not an established market gap.

## Research limits
No competitor mystery shopping, local interviews or verified current local dues. Search results included member anecdotes and proprietary-looking materials; these were not used as evidence or copied. Provider testimonials were not treated as causal performance studies. Next research should obtain actual local alternatives only after geography is selected.
