# 02 --- Referral Wheel Comparison and Evolution

## What the prior Referral Wheel work already contained

The prior Google Drive document built around HighIQ described a
**scalable referral ecosystem**. Important elements included:

-   a central company/ecosystem;
-   complementary, noncompeting partner categories;
-   minimum economics for attractive partners;
-   recurring-revenue preference;
-   "safe / risky / off-limits" partner categories;
-   ranking categories by referral potential and integration difficulty;
-   an ideal partner profile;
-   a **trigger moment** indicating when a customer needs another
    partner;
-   a simple referral script;
-   defined handoff mechanics;
-   relationship ownership;
-   commission structure;
-   expected deal size and payout;
-   partnership failure risks;
-   quality/speed/messaging controls;
-   mapping who refers to whom;
-   starting with a small number of high-value relationships;
-   a 90-day rollout.

That is already much more sophisticated than generic networking.

## Referral Wheel vs. new combined model

| Dimension | Referral Wheel | Combined Growth Network |
|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Generate reciprocal/complementary referrals | Improve member businesses and generate measurable economic value |
| Relationship design | Strategic partner ecosystem | Strategic ecosystem + peer advisory group |
| Member exclusivity | Implied through noncompetition | Formal category seat can become a key benefit |
| Referral triggers | Core concept | Core software object/workflow |
| Meetings | Not necessarily central | Structured recurring operating rhythm |
| Curriculum | Not required | Modular growth system |
| Accountability | Limited | Explicit commitments and follow-up |
| Implementation | Partner-dependent | Built into sessions and/or optional services |
| Data | Could track referrals | Tracks referrals + growth KPIs + experiments + ROI |
| SaaS opportunity | Referral management | Full member/chapter operating system |
| Monetization | Commissions/revenue sharing/partnership economics | Membership + services + software + later licensing |
| Retention | Depends heavily on referral flow | Multiple retention mechanisms |

## The most important insight

Do **not** replace the Referral Wheel. Make it a core module of the larger system.

The Referral Wheel can be the **economic network layer** underneath the Growth Council.

Each member has:
- Ideal Customer Profile;
- "listen for" trigger phrases/events;
- services offered;
- exclusions;
- geographic/service area;
- average customer value;
- preferred introduction format;
- response SLA;
- referral history;
- close rate;
- referred revenue;
- trust/quality indicators.

The software can then show the wheel as a living graph rather than a static diagram.

## Example

A remodeling contractor hears that a client plans to refinance after renovations. That is a trigger for the mortgage member.

A realtor learns that a homeowner needs major work before listing. That triggers contractor, junk removal, painter, cleaner, staging, or landscaping relationships.

A CPA hears that a client is hiring rapidly. That could trigger payroll, HR, benefits, insurance, recruiting, or legal services.

The key is that members are trained to recognize **events**, not simply memorize what everyone sells.

## New opportunity: Referral Intelligence

The software could eventually create a structured "referral intelligence" layer:
- Who should refer to whom?
- Which trigger events create the best referrals?
- Which member pairs have high theoretical overlap but few referrals?
- Which referrals close?
- Which categories produce high downstream value?
- Which member is contributing heavily but receiving little?
- Where is the network missing a category that would complete a high-value chain?

This can become an important product differentiator.

## A better conceptual model

Think of the business as three interconnected engines:

**Referral Engine** --- turns relationships and trigger events into opportunities.

**Growth Engine** --- turns business data/problems into experiments and improvements.

**Accountability Engine** --- turns ideas into assigned actions, measurements and results.

The SaaS platform is the operating system connecting all three.
