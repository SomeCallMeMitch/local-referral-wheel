## Highest priorities and mitigations
| Risk | Early signal | Mitigation / response |
|---|---|---|
| #7 Value ends after introductions | Members bypass admin and ask to stop meetings | Compare paid setup-only service with recurring coordination; don't manufacture recurring work |
| #11 Sparse triggers | Members describe hypothetical rather than recent situations | Reconstruct real events before recruitment; change categories or event focus |
| #21 Fee leakage | Unexplained lost-status deals; reconciliation resistance | Agree limited attribution before introduction; invoice only documented collected amounts; stop model if audit cost destroys margin |
| #26 Founder dependence | Only founder's own contacts produce outcomes | Track origin by source; substitute coordinator after pilot; defer chapters |
| #4 Underpaid founder | Hour log rises while dues flat | Cap support; minimum membership floor; raise price only with paid evidence; remove labor-heavy layer |
| #22 Inappropriate fee arrangement | Unclear licenses, payer or regulated transaction | Review specific arrangement before charging; exclude transaction-linked compensation pending resolution |

Other material controls: reference checks and immediate pause for serious complaints (#9); disclose conflicts and preserve customer choice (#18); private event-based reports rather than inflated group totals (#28). Local counsel/insurance review is a launch dependency, not completed work. A membership label does not sanitize a payment that functions as a referral inducement.

Stop expanding while retention or contribution economics are unresolved. A stalled pilot is cheaper than duplicating a broken chapter.
