## Implementation layer: five candidate modules, not a course
Only referral/estimate follow-up is included in A, as a 20-minute exercise replacing part of the monthly agenda once. The other four are options for C or a later comparison. Do not promise all five in the pilot.

| Module | Problem / target | Workshop activity and implemented asset | Baseline → result metric | Time to evidence | Software need / facilitator burden |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referral/estimate follow-up | Unanswered estimates in service firms | Owner selects 5 eligible outstanding estimates; sends approved follow-ups during session; sets two reminders | Age/number pending → responses, appointments, collected wins | 1–4 weeks; revenue may lag | Existing email/CRM; 1 hour shared prep + 20 minutes group + 10 minutes/member check |
| Dormant customer reactivation | Existing customers forgotten | Segment 20 eligible customers; send suitable invitation through existing permissioned channel | Contacted/untreated comparable group if feasible → incremental response and contribution | 2–6 weeks | Existing customer list/email; 2 hours prep + up to 15 minutes/member support |
| Customer referral requests | Good service never turns into introductions | Identify 5 satisfied eligible customers, send an original request with opt-in introduction instructions | Prior requests/intros → accepted qualified intros | 2–4 weeks | Email and tally; 1 hour prep + 10 minutes/member |
| Honest review requests | Good work lacks visible proof | Send neutral request to all eligible customers in a selected completed-job cohort; no positive-review condition | Requests/reviews → completed reviews, no invented revenue link | 1–4 weeks | Existing review link; 1 hour prep + 10 minutes/member |
| Repeat-service reminders | Appropriate maintenance forgotten | Select one due-service cohort and send authorized reminders | Due customers/bookings → incremental bookings and collected contribution | 2–8 weeks | Calendar/customer list; 2 hours prep + 15 minutes/member |

All burden estimates are assumptions. If records/consent are missing, select a different exercise or scope a separate preparation service; no unbounded cleanup inside dues. Owners approve and send their own messages. No mass outreach performed in this session. Avoid group sharing of future prices/customer-specific commercial terms; see regulatory research.
