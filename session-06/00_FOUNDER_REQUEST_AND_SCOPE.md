## Scope interpretation

This message specifically authorizes public-market research on real Benicia-area companies. It modifies the earlier restriction against producing real prospect lists for this research session. It does not authorize contacting, recruiting, interviewing or making representations to any business. “Finding owners to speak with” means identifying likely owners or decision-makers from public business sources for possible later consideration; no private contact information is collected.

The governing project remains an evaluation of options. A company appearing here is a research candidate, not an endorsement, approved partner or claim that it would participate. Reviews are indicators to audit, not proof of fit, demand, willingness to pay or legal permission to pay commissions.

The founder’s selection criteria are:

- Benicia first, with nearby companies only when they genuinely serve Benicia.
- Review evidence about responsiveness, listening, guidance, communication, problem resolution, follow-through and post-sale service—not star averages alone.
- Owner-involved or otherwise accessible firms may be preferable to the largest company.
- Complementary partners should have useful referral triggers and serve a similar customer without duplicating each other’s work.
- The operating model should remain compatible with brief weekly partner conversations, low urgent support, no employee-management requirement and commission income rather than monthly member fees.
