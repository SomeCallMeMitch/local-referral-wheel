# 04 — Scenario stress tests: five concepts, fifty branches
2026-09-13. Simulations are reasoned scenarios, not observed outcomes. Codes: D single-buyer portfolio; I handoff reliability; B hybrid wheel; G anchor-funded network; C success-only wheel. Amounts such as $1,000/$25,000 describe hypothetical job sizes only.

Read each row as move → countermove → next observable branch. Survival is conditional, not a promise. Severe misconduct pauses routing regardless of commercial benefit.

## Cross-model strategic moves
If no one will fund dry-period work and success fees cannot cover it, abandon recurring management for that segment. If direct relationships mature successfully, offer finite activation rather than contractual captivity. If one source is consistently disproportionate, redesign payer roles rather than impose fairness by quota. If good providers are full, sell capacity-aware routing only where permitted and useful; more leads is the wrong promise.

## What the simulations reject
C is not low-risk simply because recipients pay only on wins. B is not robust simply because it has two revenue lines. F cannot rescue founder-dependent sourcing. D/I appear less reliant on policing future relationships, but can still fail when clients can do the job cheaply themselves.
