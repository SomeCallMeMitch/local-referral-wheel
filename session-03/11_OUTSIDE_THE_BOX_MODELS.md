# 11 — What would we invent without the wheel?
2026-09-13. These are original business hypotheses using the founder's connector, marketing and systems strengths. No claim of uniqueness or demand. The point is a better buyer/job, not renaming a membership.

| Alternative | Buyer and painful job | Product/output / payment hypothesis | Why economics or attribution could improve | Failure mode / cheapest test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Partnership transition service | Firm whose founder-held alliances weakened after delegation | Finite sponsor/champion handover, trigger review, responsibility map and first live handoffs; fixed project fee, optional monitoring | Directly targets historical failure; paid deliverables rather than owning relationships forever | Client's staff lack incentives/authority; test one pair through deliberate founder-to-champion handoff |
| 2 Referral recovery desk | Firm with existing ignored or stale partner introductions | Workload-bounded queue service, reminders/escalations and outcome report; retainer | Existing queue makes workload measurable; less need to generate demand or police organic relationships | Too few stale items or staff can solve cheaply; audit small queue before paid month |

| 4 Supplier customer-success ecosystem | Supplier wants customers to succeed using complementary services | Curated vetted service connections plus adoption/problem routing; supplier-funded operating service | Anchor may benefit from customer retention, not referral commissions | Incremental retention hard to prove; test one recurring customer problem and supplier renewal |
| 5 Capacity/overflow exchange | Good providers with jobs they cannot serve and buyers needing suitable availability | Permissioned capacity check and warm transfer; service retainer or permissible fixed outcome fee | Solves an immediate bottleneck; quality/capacity fit clearer than generic lead generation | Legal scope, competition concerns, low quality backups; manual bounded matching without price/territory coordination |

## Strongest adjacent discovery
**Partnership transition service** may be the best entry product into D/I: it solves the specific moment when relationships stop producing after delegation. Instead of demanding lifelong founder attendance, establish who owns account knowledge, permission, review and escalation. If the process still fails, diagnose whether the business truly requires executive involvement.

A possible sequence: paid finite activation/transition → optional measured handoff operations → broader partner portfolio. Each step must be separately bought. Do not automatically upsell a retainer when the initial project solves the problem.

## Play three moves ahead
1. A founder pays for transition; champion performs well → client may need little ongoing help. Successful fixed project, case evidence with permission; offer only low-touch maintenance if desired.
2. Champion participates but referrals stop → examine authority/incentives/client overlap; don't add AI reminders until the cause is known. If sponsor won't act, stop.
3. Several firms buy the same transition → standardize original worksheets and training; delegate admin while founder handles sponsor decisions.
4. A chamber wants it across members → smaller distribution cost, but confidentiality and fee-paying buyer change. Separate contract and capacity budget.
5. Clients ask for software → check whether existing CRM setup solves it. Software purchase is a separate hypothesis.

## Compare to commission wheel
The wheel promises ongoing economic flow; a transition/installation project promises a bounded organizational result. The latter may have lower bypass risk and easier payment, but weaker recurring revenue and continual sales requirements. It is not automatically a better company. Its value is that it can be tested without proving long-term royalty enforcement.

## Keep creative options disciplined
Do not combine all eight into an agency. Use existing skills only where they solve a buyer's observed problem. Marketing/AI capability should support partnership work, not expand the included service until margins disappear.
