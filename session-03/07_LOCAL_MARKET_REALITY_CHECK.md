# 07 — Local market reality check
Research date: 2026-09-13. Public screening only; no business contacted. A directory listing is a candidate record, not a verified willing partner. No population, market-share or license census is implied.

## Method and estimated pool
Use Benicia/Vallejo chamber category listings plus provider-owned service-area pages. Deduplicate business names and distinguish physical location from advertised service coverage. Count an observed screening pool; do not extrapolate search-result counts into market size.

| Architecture | Observed candidate basis | Defensible estimate now | What has NOT been established |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2B operations | 6 non-law-firm accounting/bookkeeping candidates from a 7-entry category + 3 distinct MSP providers advertising area service | About 9 initial records to screen; fewer will fit a complementary pair | Exact service overlap, independence, active licenses where relevant, margins, willingness, room for another alliance |
| Home improvement/service | 3 roofing listings + 5 distinct plumbing/flooring/painting/electrical/cleaning candidates | About 8 named records; enough to justify checking a small pair, not an eight-seat launch | Residential scope, availability, quality, fees, replacement depth by trade |
| Property-owner coordination | 5 property-management-category firms + 4 shared service-provider candidates | About 9 records across buyer and vendor roles; not nine potential paying members | Anchor willingness, vendor freedom, fiduciary disclosures, procurement need and licensing scope |

Counts overlap between architectures and must not be added into 26 independent prospects. Qualified, willing and economically suitable partners confirmed: **zero**. These are modest observed screening estimates, not an upper bound on all businesses in the area.

## Existing alternatives
Benicia Chamber's BizNet describes a twice-monthly business-owner group. It directly challenges any claim that the area lacks small recurring networking opportunities. [BizNet](https://www.beniciachamber.com/biznet/). Vallejo also maintains an active business directory and chamber organization. [Vallejo directory](https://www.vallejochamber.com/list).

BNI's official finder exists, but current local chapter count, seat availability and dues were not verified through its dynamic results. [BNI finder](https://www.bni.com/find-a-chapter/). A 2016 chapter-issued event release documents historical Referral Zone presence in Benicia; it is **not evidence of a currently active chapter or schedule**. [Historical chapter announcement](https://www.newswire.com/news/referral-zone-bni-chapter-hosts-visitors-day-may-31-2016-at-carters-11140864). Current official roster confirmation remains a specific follow-up; do not label the area BNI-free.

Other strong substitutes: firms' existing preferred vendors, owner-managed alliances, internal account managers, existing CRM reminders, chambers and general lead channels. Their local prices/performance were not researched; compare actual prospect spending rather than invented competitor prices.

## Fragmentation and coverage
Several named firms appear in each screened category, so there is evidence of multiple candidate providers rather than one visible monopoly. This is NOT a market-share study or proof independent firms dominate chains. Regional MSP service pages demonstrate competition from outside city boundaries. A website targeting every city can duplicate the same company; count entities, not landing pages.

Vallejo/Benicia service overlap is plausible, with specific provider evidence. Fairfield/Suisun, American Canyon/Napa or bridge-crossing East Bay routes need provider-specific acceptance. No drive-time, toll or job-profit assumption should be based solely on map proximity.

## Decision
A small candidate pool appears accessible enough for discovery, especially B2B and property/service coordination. The evidence does not justify a large membership recruitment target, a claim of unmet demand, or projected referral volume. Before a live test, verify exact licenses/scope, independent identities, actual customer overlap, sponsor access and capacity. Research gaps are explicit: current BNI roster/dues, full category depth, ownership, willingness and job margins.
