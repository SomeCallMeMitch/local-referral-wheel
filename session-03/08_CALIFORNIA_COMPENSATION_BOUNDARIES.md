# 08 — California compensation boundaries
Practical screening dated 2026-09-13; not legal advice or approval of a contract. The operator's actual conduct matters more than "connector," "marketing," "dues" or "success fee" labels. Confirm sector, payer, activities, licenses, data and compensation terms before a live paid arrangement.

| Sector | Referral fee risk | Main issue / primary basis | Lower-risk direction to examine, NOT automatic safe harbor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contractors: roofing, painting, electrical, HVAC etc. | High/context-dependent | California BPC 7157 regulates inducements and certain compensation; contractor/HIS activity boundaries also matter | Defined nonlicensed administrative service only if applicable rules permit; customer contracts/pays licensed provider directly; review fee separately |
| Residential solar | High | CSLB distinguishes limited referral/contact/appointment functions from solicitation/sales/negotiation | Narrow consented introduction/appointment role within reviewed limits; avoid representing project economics or selling contract |
| Real estate | High | BPC 10137 restricts compensating unlicensed people for acts requiring a license; scope beyond introduction matters | Separate nonlicensed administrative work where allowed; specific finder analysis, no blanket exemption assumed |
| Mortgage/title/settlement | Very high for transaction-linked referral fees | RESPA Section 8 restricts referral compensation involving federally related mortgage settlement services | Exclude transaction fees from first test; genuine separately permissible services require review; dues are not a workaround |
| Insurance | High | INS 1631 requires licensing for covered solicitation, negotiation and effecting insurance contracts | Do not advise on policies or presume contingent commission rights; review exact non-sales referral/service arrangement |
| Legal services | Very high for ordinary paid recommendation | California Rule 7.2 limits compensation for recommending/securing legal services, with specified exceptions | Avoid ordinary deal commissions; bona fide permissible advertising/administration or authorized referral-service structure only after review |
| Healthcare | Very high | BPC 650 restricts referral compensation in covered professions; federal rules may also apply | Avoid patient referral fees; separate actual non-referral services reviewed on their own facts |
| Investment/financial services | High; regime-dependent | SEC adviser marketing requirements govern compensated endorsements; other licensing/transaction rules may apply | Compliance-led arrangement only; don't assume all financial services fit adviser rules or that disclosure alone is enough |
| General B2B IT/web/ordinary business services | Lower relative risk, not cleared | No universal sector commission approval established; contract, privacy, conflicts and actual activity still apply | Best first scope for reviewing ordinary partner-development/operations services; exclude regulated tax/legal/financial activity |
| Property-management anchor | High/context-dependent | Brokerage/fiduciary/procurement and contractor boundaries can intersect | Anchor purchases specifically permitted coordination; disclose vendor relationships, retain customer choice, avoid unlicensed management |

## Primary source register
- California [BPC 7157](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=7157.): compensation/inducement provisions and exceptions; does not itself clear a generic commission schedule.
- [CSLB solar lead-generation guidance](https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/Media_Room/Industry_Bulletins/2020/January_9.aspx): limited referral/contact/appointment activity contrasted with licensed sales functions.
- [CSLB HIS registration guidance](https://cslb.ca.gov/contractors/applicants/home_improvement_registration/before_applying_for_his.aspx): registration generally required for solicitation, selling, negotiation or execution of home-improvement contracts, subject to exceptions.
- California [BPC 10137](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=10137) and [DRE unlicensed-assistant guidance](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Licensees/UnlicensedAssistants.html): no assumption an administrative title permits licensed acts.
- [CFPB RESPA FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act-faqs/): settlement-referral compensation restrictions, including things of value.
- California [Insurance Code 1631](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=INS&sectionNum=1631.): licensing boundary. This alone does not answer every possible insurance finder-payment exception; that remains unresolved.
- [California State Bar Chapter 7, Rule 7.2](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/es/node/5120): current state source rather than relying only on ABA model rules.
- California [BPC 650](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=650.): referral restrictions and specified non-referral service/advertising provisions.
- [SEC investment-adviser marketing guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing): compensated endorsements can entail disclosure, oversight, agreement and disqualification requirements. It is not blanket permission for securities transaction compensation.
- California [BPC 16600](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=16600.): restraints on lawful trade require attention. No conclusion that every post-exit fee is a prohibited restraint, nor that a signed fee clause is enforceable.

## Post-exit fees and five/ten-year terms
Analyze enforceability separately from willingness to sign, affordability and collection. A narrowly earned commission paid later differs from restricting two businesses from trading unless they pay the operator indefinitely. Duration, scope, economic burden, actual activity and applicable law matter. This pass does not decide enforceability of a hypothetical five- or ten-year agreement.

Ask counsel to compare:
1. Recorded pre-exit opportunities with a normal collection period.
2. Named introduced customer/contract payments over a finite defined term.
3. Declining relationship-derived fees on future new customers.
4. Negotiated buyout instead of recurring reporting.
5. Non-circumvention, audit, dispute and termination terms; ensure they are not punitive substitutes for value.

Do not capitalize hypothetical royalties into unit economics. Do not use courts or collections as the recurring-value mechanism. Even enforceable terms can destroy partner trust or cost more to administer than they earn.

## Practical gate
Provide the exact service description, not "a referral wheel." Identify who selects providers, speaks to prospects, qualifies needs, discusses pricing, negotiates, signs contracts, collects customer money and invoices the referral charge. Include sample fee calculations and repeat/exit rules. Ask for a permissible scope and compensation opinion for that specific combination.

If transaction compensation is inappropriate, changing to a retainer only helps if the retained service and its purpose are independently permissible. A retainer paid to induce prohibited referrals is not made safe by being flat.

## Screening conclusion
Ordinary B2B partner operations appears simpler to examine first than a wheel monetizing mortgage, patient, legal or insurance transactions. This is a comparative screening inference. All paid tests still need their specific operating scope and terms checked; none have been approved in this session.
