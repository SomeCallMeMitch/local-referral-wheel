# 10 — Software and AI leverage
2026-09-13. No software built. Estimates below are hypotheses to measure with a stopwatch, not product claims. Software can preserve ownership, detect stale records and calculate agreed fees. It cannot supply sponsor commitment, client trust, permission, recipient capacity or willingness to pay.

## Feature decisions
Estimates assume roughly 20 active introductions/month in one unit. Minutes saved are gross before setup, exceptions and review. Overlap is substantial; do NOT add all rows as independent savings.

## Realistic savings example
Suppose manual status/reporting takes 4 hours/month. Existing tools reduce repeat entry/reminders by 60 minutes, but validation/error correction takes 20 minutes: net 40 minutes saved. At an assumed $50/hour, value is about $33/month before tool costs. Four hours setup takes six months to repay at this rate, excluding subscriptions and maintenance.

Do not turn gross 30-minute savings in five overlapping rows into a claimed 2.5 hours. Log current task time, changed time, errors and member response. Custom development is hard to justify on one small unit's admin savings alone.

## What AI cannot fix
When a sponsor delegates to a champion with no authority or motivation, AI can make the ignored task more visible. It cannot grant authority or change priorities. Product design should preserve named sponsor, operational owner and escalation. A "relationship health score" must never substitute for talking with the partner.

## Pre-contact notes: potentially useful, not default
Only send after prospect consent to the introduction and using an authorized sender. Explain that a named provider will contact them about the requested need; do not imply personal handwriting by someone who did not write it. For urgent jobs a mailed note is too slow. Test digital introduction first; compare notes only when timing supports them.

Measure accepted contact, delay, unsubscribes/complaints, response quality and net contribution after postage/production/time. The note must not become a marketing-list enrollment, inducement or unconsented disclosure. No notes or campaigns sent in this session.

## Conditional future product
Minimum: partner/scope, consented opportunity, responsible owner/next action, status, permitted fee evidence and capacity. Add relationship/contract identifiers only when recurring/exit terms actually require them. Existing CRM/billing can remain systems of record.

Standalone software requires independent operators who already have referral flow and pay to solve the same recurring friction. Three paying design partners with similar needs would be a useful early signal, not market validation. Don't build a platform to repair a network with no demand.

## Build gate
Repeated paid operation, reliable data, measurable recurring friction, viable recipient/operator economics, clear privacy permissions and existing-tool limitations. Demand for a useful service is not automatically demand for its software.
