2026-09-13. "Fatal" means fatal to a specified configuration if the condition is demonstrated, not to every adjacent business. Unknown is not permission to assume away the risk.

## Strongest hostile case
The operator creates a relationship once, then needs endless prompting and billing to extract a modest share from partners who prefer direct dealing. Low-margin local jobs cannot pay for that labor; stronger firms already have sources; high-ticket sectors introduce regulatory complexity; automation only sends ignored reminders. A broad tail agreement then becomes the substitute for continuing value.

If observation supports this case, do not pursue a commission-wheel business. Sell finite partner activation or a narrow operational service only if someone pays for it. There is no requirement to salvage "the wheel."

## What would make us more optimistic
Voluntary repeated referrals across independent pairs; positive recipient contribution after total fees; paid service renewals; low disputed-fee dollars; operator hours within scope; sponsor/champion handoff preserves behavior; partners describe specific work the operator performs that they do not want to own.

## Early stop rules (assumptions, not norms)
Stop routing on serious credible misconduct pending review. Stop the current offer after two successive review periods below the agreed operator earnings floor without a funded learning rationale. Do not expand while customer benefit, collection quality or delegated operation remains unknown. Never hide one member's poor results behind a superstar's revenue.
