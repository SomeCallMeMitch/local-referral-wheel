# Session 6 — Basic Benicia contender search

Completed 2026-09-14. Public-market research was specifically authorized for this session. No company or owner was contacted.

## Main result

The best initial **service and relationship fit** is a planned home-refresh trio:

1. Home Living Window Fashions — owners Shem and Melody Isaac.
2. Benicia Floor Company — owners Wayne and Judy Bottini.
3. Solano Painting Bros — general partner Sal A. Sammartino.

Public reviews and profiles contain recurring evidence of patient guidance, clear communication, respect for the customer/home, careful work, issue resolution and follow-through. Their scopes are complementary: window treatments, flooring and painting. Each can encounter a customer need for the other two during a planned move-in or home refresh. Home Living has the strongest cross-source client-care evidence; Benicia Floor’s platform counts conflict and need reconciliation; Solano Painting looks small and owner-involved but needs a direct current review/license audit.

This is not yet a cleared commission network. CSLB explicitly warns that contractor referral or kickback fees may violate California Business and Professions Code §7157. CSLB separately says registered Home Improvement Salespersons can represent multiple contractor employers after required notifications. That possible path is a sales-employment model, not an informal referral fee, and it could increase regulation, founder sales responsibility and founder dependence. The exact structure needs further primary-source and professional analysis before any commission is treated as available.

## Secondary findings

- Simplified Spaces is a strategically promising source/satellite because organizers hear about planned home needs early, but its favorable evidence is mainly provider-selected testimonials and needs independent review verification.
- Shandrika Powell has a strong service profile and can be a high-value real-estate/staging/design hub. Existing-network strength, scope overlap and regulated compensation make her a separate model, not an automatic fourth member.
- Hire My Husband has strong visible review volume but broad handyman/painting/preparation scope. It is a better satellite for declined small work than a category-clean core partner.
- B’Santi Cleaning’s visible review score mixes cleaning-service and vacation-rental-stay experiences, so the headline rating is not reliable for this decision.
- The home-transition cluster is promising but currently has weaker Benicia evidence and likely smaller per-job commission capacity.
- A Benicia event cluster has credible service-oriented firms, but venue gatekeeping, seasonality and event urgency make it less aligned with the founder than the home-refresh hypothesis.
