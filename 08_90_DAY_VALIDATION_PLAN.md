# 08 --- 90-Day Validation Plan

## Objective

Prove or disprove the core behavior before investing heavily in software, branding, curriculum, or licensing.

## Phase 1 --- Weeks 1--2: Design the minimum offer

Create only:
- working name;
- one-page offer;
- ideal-member criteria;
- category map;
- pilot price;
- simple confidentiality/conduct terms;
- member intake;
- sample dashboard mockup;
- first two meeting agendas;
- referral trigger worksheet;
- one implementation workshop.

Do not build a full app.

## Phase 2 --- Weeks 2--5: Customer discovery + presale

Build a list of 50--100 likely members in one practical geographic market.

Interview/approach owners and track:
- which promise gets attention;
- objections;
- preferred meeting cadence;
- willingness to pay;
- whether referral exclusivity matters;
- whether dashboard/ROI tracking matters;
- desired growth problems;
- category conflicts.

### Validation thresholds

These are decision rules, not industry facts:
- 0--2 willing to pay: do not build; revise.
- 3--4: signal exists; diagnose objections.
- 5--7: enough for a pilot.
- 8--10: strong signal; launch.
- waiting list in categories: evidence that exclusivity has value.

Collect money or a real deposit where practical. Verbal enthusiasm is weak validation.

## Phase 3 --- Weeks 5--8: Run manually

Use simple forms/spreadsheets/manual dashboards.

Test:
- referral trigger profiles;
- one growth workshop;
- one council meeting;
- commitments;
- referral logging;
- outcome logging;
- weekly/biweekly follow-up.

Observe where members naturally ask for software.

## Phase 4 --- Weeks 8--12: Measure

For each member:
- attendance;
- referrals given;
- referrals received;
- pipeline;
- closed value;
- actions completed;
- experiment result;
- subjective value;
- renewal intent.

Ask: **If the pilot ended today, what would you miss most?**

That answer should guide the product.

## Build/no-build gates

Build an MVP only if:
- members repeatedly use at least 2--3 workflows;
- data entry can be made simple;
- at least several members intend to renew;
- members can articulate value without prompting;
- facilitator workload is economically viable.

## First MVP

If validated, build only:
- login/member profile;
- directory;
- referral triggers;
- send/track referral;
- commitments;
- simple ROI dashboard.

Everything else can wait.

## Local-market research follow-up

A separate research pass should identify the actual candidate categories and businesses in the initial target geography, map existing BNI/TAB/Vistage/chamber competition, and estimate how many businesses meet the desired maturity threshold.

That should be done before selecting the final chapter boundaries.
