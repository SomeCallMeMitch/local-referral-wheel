# Trigger library

> Latest direction (2026-09-13): Vallejo, California and surrounding cities; founder role is connector; every fee/amount remains illustrative, with participating-business profitability first. See [current connector and fee-design update](../strategy/09_VALLEJO_CONNECTOR_AND_FEE_DESIGN.md). This update overrides conflicting earlier defaults.
These are original examples for discussion, not promises of demand. Ask permission before any named introduction. No pressure, diagnosis outside competence, or sharing sensitive life-event details.

| Event / listener | Listen-for language | Basic fit questions | Possible recipient | Exclusion / urgency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC service visit | "This room never gets comfortable" | Has it been assessed? Would you like a specialist introduction? | HVAC specialist; qualified insulation provider if separately assessed | Do not diagnose building/safety issues casually |
| Plumbing visit | "We will repaint after this is repaired" | Is the source fixed? What area needs work? | Painter/flooring after necessary remediation | Active water damage needs appropriate urgent response |
| Painting estimate | "We're replacing these floors too" | What timing? Which rooms? | Flooring specialist | Coordinate order; separate contracts |
| Flooring quote | "The room needs clearing" | What must move? Temporary storage needed? | Moving/junk-removal provider | Clarify disposal authorization |
| Landscaping visit | "We want better outdoor lighting" | Is electrical work required? What service area? | Licensed electrician | No electrical recommendations beyond scope |
| Electrical service | "We just moved in" | What other planned tasks? Would a specific introduction help? | Painter/HVAC/cleaner | Customer selects priorities |
| Cleaning visit | "We need someone reliable for maintenance" | What specific task? How soon? | Relevant qualified trade | Cleaner does not inspect/diagnose |
| Junk-removal inquiry | "We are getting ready to renovate" | Has a contractor been chosen? What scope? | Relevant specialist | General contractor overlaps must be explicit |
| MSP review | "We're hiring several people" | What systems/processes need capacity? | Payroll/HR provider | Do not share employee information |
| Bookkeeping review | "Invoices take too long to get paid" | Is the issue process or payment handling? | CRM/workflow specialist | No promises about financing/collections law |
| Office supplier | "We are opening another location" | Lease signed? What stage? | IT, signage, commercial cleaning | Separate regulated brokerage/legal needs |
| Recruiter | "New hires need onboarding" | Who owns onboarding? What deadline? | HR/process provider | Confidential candidate information excluded |
| Customer service conversation | "My parent needs help maintaining the house" | Who can authorize work? Is the customer comfortable with contact? | Maintenance/cleaning provider | Avoid collecting medical details; no coercion |
| Business owner discussion | "I want to retire in two years" | Would you like contact with your own qualified adviser? | Legal/tax/succession professional | Outside initial success-fee scope |

Intro template: "You mentioned [specific need]. [Provider] handles [relevant scope] in [area]. Would you like me to introduce you by [channel]? You are free to choose anyone." Where a financial relationship exists, explain it plainly before the introduction. This template does not make an otherwise prohibited referral fee permissible.

Each member replaces generic phrases with three actual examples from their last 60–90 days. Specific evidence beats a large theoretical library.
